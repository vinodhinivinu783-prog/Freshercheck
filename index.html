🚩 Common Scam Signs

❌ Registration fee
❌ Gmail/Yahoo recruiter
❌ WhatsApp-only hiring
❌ Direct job without interview
❌ Unrealistic salary
❌ Urgent payment request

✅ Genuine Job Signs

✅ Official company website
✅ Official career page
✅ Proper interview process
✅ No recruitment fee
✅ Verifiable recruiter
✅ Company details available

🛡️ Remember

A real company name does not automatically mean the job offer is real. Scammers often impersonate famous companies.
